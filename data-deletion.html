HomeVault Data Deletion

Last updated: 2026-11-24

HomeVault stores user data primarily on the device. Users can delete their data at any time.

Delete data yourself

  • Delete individual items and attachments directly in the app.
  • Delete all app data by clearing app storage in Android settings.
  • Delete all local data by uninstalling the app.

Request deletion help

If you need help deleting your data, contact: savmidal@gmail.com

Please include:

  • App name: HomeVault
  • Package name: com.savdal.homevault
  • Your request details

Third-party billing data

Premium purchases are processed by Google Play. Purchase records are managed under your Google account and Google Play policies.
