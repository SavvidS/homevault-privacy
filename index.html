HomeVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

HomeVault (“the app”) helps users manage home inventory, warranties, receipts, and reminders. This Privacy Policy explains what data is handled and how.

1. Data We Handle

The app may store the following information entered by the user:

  • Item details (name, brand, model, serial number, category, notes)
  • Purchase details (date, retailer, price)
  • Warranty details and reminder preferences
  • Optional attachments (photos, receipts, warranty documents)
  • Premium purchase status (for feature entitlement)

2. How Data Is Stored

HomeVault stores user data locally on the device by default. Backup/export files are created only when the user explicitly triggers backup/export actions.

3. Data Sharing

HomeVault does not sell personal data. HomeVault does not share user data with third-party advertisers.

4. Payments

Premium purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. HomeVault does not collect or store full payment card details. Payment handling is governed by Google Play terms and policies.

5. Permissions

The app may request permissions such as:

  • Camera/media access (for adding item photos or documents)
  • Notification permission (for warranty reminders)
  • Network access (for billing and optional operations that require connectivity)

6. Data Deletion

Users can delete data in-app (for individual items) and can remove all local app data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

7. Children

HomeVault is not directed to children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page.
